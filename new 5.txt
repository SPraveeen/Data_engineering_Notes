🔹 Task 3: Statistical Tests
1️⃣ Levene’s Test (Equality of Variance)
from scipy.stats import levene

levene(df[df["CHAS"]==0]["MEDV"],
       df[df["CHAS"]==1]["MEDV"])

📌 Interpretation:

p > 0.05 → Equal variances

p < 0.05 → Variances differ

Management Meaning:
Checks if price variability differs between river and non-river homes.

2️⃣ T-Test (Difference in MEDV by CHAS)
from scipy.stats import ttest_ind

ttest_ind(df[df["CHAS"]==0]["MEDV"],
          df[df["CHAS"]==1]["MEDV"])

📌 Interpretation:

p < 0.05 → Significant price difference

p > 0.05 → No significant difference

Management Meaning:
Determines if being near the Charles River significantly impacts house prices.

3️⃣ ANOVA (MEDV across AGE Groups)
from scipy.stats import f_oneway

f_oneway(df[df["AGE_GROUP"]==df["AGE_GROUP"].unique()[0]]["MEDV"],
         df[df["AGE_GROUP"]==df["AGE_GROUP"].unique()[1]]["MEDV"],
         df[df["AGE_GROUP"]==df["AGE_GROUP"].unique()[2]]["MEDV"])

📌 Interpretation:

p < 0.05 → At least one age group differs in price

p > 0.05 → No significant difference

Management Meaning:
Shows whether house age significantly affects value.

4️⃣ Pearson Correlation (NOX vs INDUS)
from scipy.stats import pearsonr

pearsonr(df["NOX"], df["INDUS"])

📌 Interpretation:

r close to +1 → Strong positive relationship

p < 0.05 → Statistically significant

Management Meaning:
Industrial areas tend to have higher pollution.

5️⃣ Linear Regression: DIS → MEDV
import statsmodels.api as sm

X = sm.add_constant(df["DIS"])
model = sm.OLS(df["MEDV"], X).fit()
model.summary()

📌 Key Interpretation Points for Management:

Slope (DIS coefficient):

Positive → Houses farther from employment centers have higher value.

Negative → Houses farther away are cheaper.

p-value:

< 0.05 → Distance significantly affects price.

R²:

Shows how much of price variation is explained by distance.
